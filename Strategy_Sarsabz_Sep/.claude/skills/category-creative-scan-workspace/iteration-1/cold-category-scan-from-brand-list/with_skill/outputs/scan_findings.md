## Addendum: cadence corrected for pinned posts (added by hand)

Instagram serves up to three **pinned** posts at the top of a grid, and the collector
reads them in grid order. Section 5 above therefore stretches each brand's window back
to a pinned post that may be months old, and understates the banks badly (HBL reads as
0.6 posts/week when it published five posts in six days).

Recomputed after dropping the first three grid positions per brand:

| Brand | Pinned dates (excluded) | Unpinned window | Unpinned posts/week |
|---|---|---|---|
| JS Bank | 2026-06-01 x3 | 26 Aug only (1 day) | ~63 |
| UBL | 2026-07-24, 08-27, 08-27 | 26-27 Aug (2 days) | ~31 |
| Meezan Bank | 2026-08-21, 08-21, 08-20 | 26-27 Aug (2 days) | ~31 |
| Bank Alfalah | 2026-08-08, 08-05, 07-08 | 25-27 Aug (3 days) | ~21 |
| HBL | 2026-08-18, 06-04, 04-06 | 22-27 Aug (6 days) | ~10.5 |
| Easypaisa | 2026-05-13, 01-16, 08-26 | 18 Jul - 22 Aug (36 days) | ~1.7 |
| SadaPay | 2026-04-17, 01-22, 2025-10-20 | 11 Jun - 21 Aug (72 days) | ~0.9 |

Read it as an order of magnitude, not a precise rate: for the four banks the twelve
collected posts fit inside one to three days, which means the true rate is at or above
the figure shown and the sample is a snapshot of a couple of days, not of a month. The
shape of the finding is safe either way: **the banks publish at roughly ten to thirty
times the rate of the two fintechs, and the fintechs are the ones with a recognisable
voice.**
