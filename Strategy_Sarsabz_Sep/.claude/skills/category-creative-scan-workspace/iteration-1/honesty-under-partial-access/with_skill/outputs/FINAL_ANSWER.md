## Why there's no share-of-voice number here

Instagram serves a logged-out browser about 12 posts per profile and gates the rest behind a login wall. So I got exactly 12 posts from every brand — Al Meezan (81.8K followers) and Lakson (428 followers) contributed identical volume. Any SoV chart built on that would be measuring my collector's ceiling, not the category. It would also be organic-only: nothing here says anything about paid spend, reach or media weight.

Two ways to actually get it, if you need it:
- **An authenticated session** (instaloader with your own cookies, on a secondary account — automated collection breaches Instagram's ToS and the account holding the cookie is the one that gets blocked). That gives total post counts, likes, comments and full history.
- **Apify's Instagram Scraper**, a couple of dollars for a job this size, no risk to your account. Faster if you're on a deadline.
- For *paid* SoV, neither helps — that's Meta Ad Library, manual, and it shows no spend figures for commercial advertisers.

### The four things that actually matter

**1. 65% of the category's posts prove nothing.** 110 of 168 carry no proof device at all. Where a number does appear (42 posts) it's almost always the fund's own trailing return against its benchmark — and three of AL Habib's return cards and one of AKD's show the fund *behind* its benchmark. **2. The substitute is competing directly for money market fund money and the AMCs are not answering.

**3.
