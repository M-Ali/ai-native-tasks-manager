## The finding

**Nobody in this category is over forty.**

`anti_ageing` — the largest value pool in global skincare — is claimed by **0 of 84 posts**, and the two brands that would naturally own it (Olay, Cetaphil) have no Pakistan account to claim it from. The occupied centre is brightening (13 posts, 9 of them purely aspirational) and cleansing (11). **74% of the category's output offers no proof of anything** — no statistic, no test, no before/after, no expert. Three posts in 84 carry a number at all. The one visible before/after in the entire scan is a repost on Dermaceutical's feed, shot by a creator.

The vacant territory is a **proven claim made to an adult**. Conatural has already taken the honesty position rhetorically — "We put ACTIVS in. Not PROMISES." / "NOT A MIRACLE. Just good ingredients doing their job." — and has published zero evidence behind it in twelve posts. That gap stays open for exactly as long as it takes someone to publish one credible number.

## Three things worth knowing before you use this

- **Nivea Pakistan is dormant.** Its twelve most recent posts run 28 May – 1 August **2022**. Nothing for 1,487 days. Its slide reads as a finding about who is actually contesting this market, not as a gap in the sample.
- **Two cadence figures in the findings file are distorted by pinned posts.** POND'S shows 1.0/week and Dermaceutical 0.2/week because both pin an older post that stretches the date span. Excluding the pins: POND'S ≈3.2/week, Dermaceutical ≈6.3/week. Conatural (12.0/week) and Saeed Ghani (5.6/week) are genuinely the most active.
- **32 posts carry an `unclear` code on at least one dimension** — mostly audience, where a packshot gives no human signal to read. Coded honestly rather than guessed.
