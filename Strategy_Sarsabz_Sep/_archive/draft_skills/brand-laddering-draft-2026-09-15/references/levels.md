# The six levels, the factors that populate them, and a worked example

Source framework: *Levels of Branding* — branding as a spectrum from functional elements (name, attributes) to
representational ones (values, philosophy). The levels are **depth of meaning, not stages of quality**. A brand
operating well at Level 2 in a low-involvement category is not failing.

## Level definitions

### 1. Product identification — "I am here"
The brand exists as a name, with no significant association beyond recognition. Common in low-involvement
categories where risk is low and the first brand recalled gets bought. **Factors:** name, logo, distinctive
assets, visual identity, heritage as recognition, presence, availability.

Heritage sits here, not at Level 5. A brand's age proves it exists and is familiar; it does not by itself say
what the brand believes. Treating heritage as a value is the most common laddering error.

### 2. Product attributes — "Reason to buy me"
What the brand *has*: features, specifications, ingredients, price, coverage, formulation. Differentiation is
claimed on the thing itself. Attributes are copyable, and in most categories this rung is crowded.

### 3. Product benefits — "Here is what I can do for you"
What the attribute *does for the buyer*: time saved, effort reduced, yield gained, peace of mind, a task made
easier. The translation from attribute to benefit is where most categories stop.

### 4. Psychological associations — "I am like you; you are like me"
What choosing the brand *says about the buyer*: smart, in control, respected, a good parent, a progressive
farmer, a modern consumer. Read from casting, setting and register as much as from copy.

### 5. Human values / way of life — "I think like you do"
What the brand *believes*: fairness, progress, inclusion, community, self-improvement, independence. A value is
only at Level 5 if the brand's behaviour demonstrates it; stated in a film alone it reads as Level 1 decoration.

### 6. Mission / philosophy — "I change things you would like to"
The change the brand exists to create, beyond its own commercial interest: digital inclusion, food security,
democratised technology. Rare, and rarer still that it is believed. Requires evidence at every level beneath it.

## Factor pools per level

Use these to populate the inventory. They are prompts, not a fixed list — add category-specific factors and
mark them `†`.

| Level | Common factors |
|---|---|
| 1 | Brand name · logo · visual identity · distinctive assets · heritage · availability · recall · familiarity |
| 2 | Specification · formulation · ingredients · price · packages · coverage · technology · distribution · service channels · certification · published terms |
| 3 | Better performance · reliability · saves time · reduces effort · makes a task easier · peace of mind · value for money · productivity · a problem solved |
| 4 | Smart choice · in control · confidence · security · status · belonging · pride · achievement · respected as a customer · responsible parent · progressive peer · successful professional |
| 5 | Progress · fairness · family wellbeing · freedom · equality · ambition · inclusion · community · modernity · self-improvement · education · opportunity · togetherness · independence · sustainability |
| 6 | Democratising access · connecting communities · enabling livelihoods · national food security · improving education · changing how a category is understood |

**Unsupported factors** are the ones that belong to other categories and would not be believed in this one.
Listing them explicitly is what keeps a purpose slide from drifting into borrowed language.

## Mapping coded claims to levels

Start from the scan's claim vocabulary and map each value once, in writing. Two categories, worked:

**Fixed broadband**

| Coded claim | Level |
|---|---|
| `brand_corporate`, heritage, national days | 1 |
| `price_affordability`, speed tiers, device bundles, coverage | 2 |
| `convenience_digital`, entertainment at home, business continuity, help when needed | 3 |
| pride via sport/celebrity, nostalgia, gamer identity, aspiration | 4 |
| CSR, national development, social responsibility | 5 |
| (none observed) | 6 |

**Fertilizer** (for a scan using the vocabulary in `code_window.py`)

| Coded claim | Level |
|---|---|
| `brand_goodwill`, sponsorship, calendar/festive | 1 |
| `product_quality`, `rewards_prizes`, granule, formulation, anti-counterfeit sticker | 2 |
| `yield_increase`, `advisory_service`, cost per acre, soil health | 3 |
| `farmer_recognition` (what a farmer is seen to be), progressive-farmer identity | 4 |
| `women_empowerment`, `national_identity`, community | 5 |
| food security, farmer prosperity as a stated mission | 6 |

Note how `farmer_recognition` can sit at 4 or 5 depending on the execution: a salute to farmers as a group is
closer to a value; "this is what a progressive farmer uses" is psychological association. Decide per row where
the difference matters, and record the rule.

## Worked example: Pakistani fixed broadband

336 coded Instagram posts, 14 brands, plus 976 customer comments.

**Placement (computed, mean level per brand):** StormFiber 2.6, Zong 2.8, Optix 2.9, Jazz 2.9, Tapmad 3.0,
Multinet 3.2, Jazz Business 3.3, Transworld 3.8, Ufone 3.9. The client (PTCL) talked mostly at Level 5 —
heritage and nation in the pride register, 14 of its 24 posts — while its modal own-post level was 1 and its
mean 2.4.

**The category's shape:** everyone past Level 1, clustered at 2-3. Nobody at Level 6. 68% of posts carried no
proof device; 0 of 336 cited an independent speed test.

**The demand side:** customers judged at Levels 2-3 — megabits delivered versus sold, the actual bill, a fault
fixed, a clean exit — and named fairness only by its absence ("fraud", "zulm").

**The verdicts:**

| Level | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 6 Purpose | `NOT NOW` | No permission; no purpose evidence anywhere in the category |
| 5 Values | `LATER` | Fairness, once proven |
| 4 Psychological | `NEXT` | Respected, in control |
| 3 Benefits | `PLACE HERE` | "Fair terms you can count on" — unclaimed, answers the customer pulse, provable by commercial decisions |
| 2 Attributes | `PROVE` | Publish terms and speed |
| 1 Identification | `HOLD` | Heritage = recognition, and 13 of 14 brands already claim it |

**The conclusion sentence:** *"PTCL is communicating roughly two levels above where it is delivering — and the
ladder does not let a brand skip."*
