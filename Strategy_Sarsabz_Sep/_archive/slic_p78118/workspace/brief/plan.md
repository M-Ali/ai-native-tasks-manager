## The ask

SLIC wants to prequalify advertising agencies for a 2026-28 roster, and will issue the
real RFP only to those that pass. Despite being called a prequalification, this is a
pitch with a paperwork gate: 70 of the 100 technical marks are a speculative campaign
(concept, competitive analysis, communication strategy, creative artworks, media mix),
and only 30 are corporate documents. Pass mark is 50, so the paperwork alone cannot
qualify us.

The gate is press and broadcast accreditation - APNS, PBA, AAP and PID - which means
SLIC is buying a full-service ATL agency, not a digital practice. Only the applicant
entity's own qualifications count (ITA 22.1, 23.3) and joint ventures are capped at nil
members, so we cannot lean on Group scale or an affiliate's accreditation.
