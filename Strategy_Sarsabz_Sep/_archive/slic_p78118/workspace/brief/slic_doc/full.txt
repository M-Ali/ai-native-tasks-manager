STRATEGY / CREATIVE TEAM — 70 MARKS
The evaluation allocates 70 marks to the following:
Campaign Concept — 15 Marks
Develop campaign concept.
Clearly articulate the core idea.
Ensure concept is relevant to SLIC.
Prepare presentation-ready rationale.
Competitive Analysis — 15 Marks
Identify relevant competitors.
Analyse their communication.
Identify gaps/opportunities.
Establish SLIC's potential communication differentiation.
Communication Strategy — 15 Marks
Define communication objective.
Define target audience.
Establish communication approach.
Develop messaging direction.
Define communication journey.
Creative Artworks — 15 Marks
Develop KV/creative direction.
Prepare required sample artworks.
Ensure quality is presentation-ready.
Ensure creative work demonstrates the proposed campaign concept.
Proposed Media Mix — 10 Marks
Define proposed media channels.
Explain role of each medium.
Demonstrate how the media mix supports the communication strategy.
