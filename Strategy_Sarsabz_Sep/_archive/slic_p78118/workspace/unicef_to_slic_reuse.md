# What transfers from the UNICEF pitch to the SLIC prequalification

Sources compared:

- `data/UNICEF_PITCH/ToR-Communication for Digital Fundraising in Pakistan.pdf` — UNICEF Pakistan CO, 8pp, start 1 Sep 2026 to 31 Dec 2027.
- `data/UNICEF_PITCH/UNICEF-Digital-Fundraising-Technical-Proposal-v6_Highlighted_Integrated.docx` — our submission, 10 Aug 2026, by **Synite Digital (Pvt.) Ltd.**, a Synergy Group company.
- `data/pq-bidding-document (2).pdf` — SLIC prequalification P78118, due 3 Sep 2026.

## 1. The two tenders are not the same animal

| | UNICEF | SLIC |
|---|---|---|
| Instrument | RFP — full technical + financial proposal | **Prequalification only** — the RFP comes later |
| Buying | A digital performance-marketing partner | A **full-service advertising agency** roster |
| Gatekeeping | Company registration + recent audit report | **APNS + PBA + AAP registration, PID enlistment** |
| Scoring | 70 technical / 30 price | 100 technical, 50 to pass, no price at this stage |
| Weight sits in | Implementation plan (35), experience (10+10) | **Speculative campaign (70)** |
| Bid entity | Synite Digital (Pvt.) Ltd. | See §2 — probably must be a different entity |

UNICEF's gate is commercial (are you registered, are you audited). SLIC's gate is
**industry accreditation for press and broadcast release** — APNS, PBA, AAP, PID. That is
a traditional media-release credential set, and nothing in the UNICEF pack evidences it.

## 2. The decision that comes before any reuse: which entity applies

SLIC ITA 22.1 and 23.3 are explicit — *"the qualifications of other related entities such as
the Applicant's subsidiaries, parent entities, affiliates, subcontractors or any other firm(s)
different from the Applicant shall not be taken into consideration."* The PDS also caps JV
members at **nil**, so no joint application.

The UNICEF proposal repeatedly reaches up into the Group for its strength — *"as part of the
Synergy Group, access to 25+ years"*, *"four offices … more than 250 professionals"*,
*"drawing upon … the wider Synergy Group's creative, strategy, media, communications … resources"*.
Under UNICEF's rules that was fine. **Under SLIC's rules that framing is worth zero**, and
using it would invite disqualification rather than marks.

So the applicant entity must hold, in its own name:

- APNS, PBA, AAP registration and PID enlistment — eligibility, plus 3 marks
- PKR 100m+ aggregate revenue over 3 years — 4 marks
- 20+ permanent employees — 4 marks
- Operational offices in Karachi, Lahore **and** Islamabad — 2 marks
- SECP private-limited incorporation — 2 marks

On the face of it that points to **Synergy Advertising (Pvt.) Ltd.**, not Synite Digital —
Synite is described as the specialist *digital* practice, incorporated 9 Feb 2018, and is the
entity least likely to hold press/broadcast accreditation. **This needs confirming against the
actual certificates before anything else is drafted.** Everything below assumes the applicant
is whichever Group entity holds the APNS/PBA/AAP/PID set.

## 4. What reuses as thinking (the 70-mark campaign)

This is where the real value is, and it is not the digital-fundraising content — it is the
audience problem underneath it.

**The UNICEF insight transfers almost intact.** §3.2 of our proposal argues Pakistani donors
face a trust deficit toward large institutions, prefer tangible and immediate benefit, and
frame giving religiously (Zakat/Sadaqah). Life insurance in Pakistan faces a structurally
identical set of barriers: very low penetration, distrust of institutions holding money for
decades, a preference for visible near-term benefit over deferred protection, and religious
reservations about conventional insurance that push buyers toward Takaful. The five-question
donor decision model we built — **TRUST / RELEVANCE / IMPACT / EASE / CONFIDENCE** — maps
onto the life-insurance purchase decision with barely a word changed. That is a ready-made
spine for SLIC's *Communication Strategy* (15 marks).

**The strongest credentials for SLIC are the ones that were secondary at UNICEF.** For a
state-owned life insurer, the closest analogues in the pack are the State Bank of Pakistan
campaigns — Raast, Roshan Digital Account, Asaan Mobile Account. Same client type (a
state-owned financial institution), same task (persuade Pakistanis to trust a formal financial
instrument with their money), and hard, quotable outcomes: 9.1M new accounts, PKR 86.7bn in
transactions, 95,000+ accounts and $1.3bn remittances, 92% TV reach. Roshan Digital Account
also proves diaspora reach — directly relevant, since overseas Pakistanis are a live segment
for State Life policies. The Polio Eradication work adds the other half: shifting entrenched,
religiously-inflected resistance using scholars, regional languages and long-form content.

**Reusable frameworks, content to be rebuilt:** the four-phase intervention model (Build Intent
→ Convert → Optimize In-Flight → Extend the Relationship), the channel-allocation and
creative-testing tables (§4.4.4), the optimization cadence, the risk-and-mitigation table
(§4.10), and the three-level QA review (§7.1). All of these are structure, and structure
travels. The *Proposed Media Mix* (10 marks) can be built on §4.4.4's logic.

**What does not transfer:** the campaign concept and the creative artworks — 30 of the 70
marks. These have to be originated for State Life. And note the medium shift: SLIC's gate is
press and broadcast accreditation, so its evaluators will likely expect an ATL-weighted answer
(print, TVC) rather than the Meta/Google-weighted mix that suited UNICEF. Lead with the
docu-drama, anthem and PSA credentials, not the ROAS tables.

## 5. Two practical cautions

**Capacity overlap.** The UNICEF engagement starts 1 September 2026; the SLIC application is
due 3 September 2026. The same senior names — Faheem Ahmed, Emaad Ishaq Khan, Ferrukh Syed —
anchor both. If SLIC prequalification converts to an RFP, check the named team can carry both
before committing CVs.

**Do not paste the UNICEF ROAS numbers into a SLIC submission unchecked.** The Indus Hospital
and Patients' Aid figures are client-confidential fundraising results. They are excellent
proof of performance discipline, but confirm release permission, and remember they answer a
question SLIC has not asked at this stage.

## 6. Bottom line

The UNICEF pack closes roughly **half the SLIC paperwork** — profile, client list, staff
particulars and CVs, tax and registration documents, foreign affiliation — and gives us a
tested strategic spine plus the two best-fitting credentials in the portfolio (SBP and Polio)
for the 70-mark campaign.

It closes **none of the accreditation gate**. APNS, PBA, AAP and PID are pass/fail, worth 3
further marks, and are the reason the entity question in §2 has to be settled first.
