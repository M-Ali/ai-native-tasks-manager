Fatima Group – One of the fastest growing conglomerates of Pakistan  
Company Background  
 
Established in 1936 as a commodity trading business, Fatima Group is one of the most diversified and 
fastest growing conglomerates in Pakistan. Our business lines include fertilizer, energy, sugar, 
mining, venture capital, information technology, real estate, trading, textile, packaging, agri-
businesses, cosmetics, cement and healthcare. 
Fatima Group endeavours to be a global player in the fertilizer industry and is in the process of 
developing projects offshore. We believe in sustained growth through ethical practices, 
benchmarking global best practices and long-term partnerships.  
2026 marks the 90th year of Fatima Group, as it stands at the threshold of a transformative era. 
Driven by decisive leadership, far-sighted vision and a commitment to sustainable growth, the Group 
continues to diversify its presence across a wide range of industries including fertilizer, energy, 
mining and minerals, technology, petroleum, aviation, beauty, healthcare and other emerging 
businesses. As Fatima Group enters its next phase of growth, it remains focused on building robust 
businesses, embracing innovation and creating long-term value for its customers, stakeholders and 
the communities it serves, while strengthening its contribution to Pakistan's economic development.

Our Brands  
1. Sarsabz: 
 
Our brand “Sarsabz” was launched in 2011 with the tagline ‘Zameen ki Jaan, Fasal ki Shaan’, having a 
vision to create high-value fertilizers that are developed after extensive research of Pakistani soils 
and weather conditions. Being the only Nitrate fertilizer manufacturer, we pride ourselves in being 
the only fertilizer brand that promises measurable results of 10% greater yield as compared to 
conventional fertilizers with a combined usage of Sarsabz Nitrophos and CAN.  
Sarsabz Fertilizers endeavour to safeguard hard work of farmers by providing them on average 10% 
higher yield compared to conventional fertilizers. The fulfilment of this promise is well documented 
by independent research and educational institutes along with government research stations that 
have collaborated with Fatima Group over the years in conducting 500+ demo plots across Pakistan 
on various crops in multiple soil and environmental conditions. 
Our key brand values are as follows: 
▪ Core Values: we stand for Growth, Progress and Innovation in Agriculture. 
▪ Brand Pillars: we are Experts in the field, Caring and farmer focused, and a One-stop solution 
for the needs of the farming community.  
 
2. Bubber Sher (Regional Brand) 
 
A regional brand that markets our conventional fertilizer Urea and DAP in North Zone. The Brand has 
a long and rich history of providing high quality product to its customers fresh out of its centrally 
located plant. It has over the years developed a special bond with its farmer base due to its 
consistent quality and excellent reputation. Over the years, it has developed a special bond with its 
consumer base making it our legacy brand. It symbolizes the traditions, values and culture of rural 
Punjab, and resides very close to the hearts of farmers.  
 
3. Pakarab CAN  
 
Produced at Pakarab Plant in Multan, Pakarab CAN has an efficient and effective supply of necessary 
nutrients to the plants with minimal losses is of significant importance and ensures healthy growth 
of the crop.

Brand Assets 
Aligned with our core values, Sarsabz takes pride in being the pioneer in Pakistan, paying 
homage to its end-users – the farmers – by designating December 18 as Farmer Day. This 
initiative of ‘Salam Kissan’ received further acclaim when the Federal Government 
officially recognized December 18 as 'Kissan Day,' to be celebrated each year. This 
accomplishment stands as a momentous achievement not only for Sarsabz but also for the 
entire farming community of Pakistan.  
 Sarsabz Kahani is another digital initiative of Sarsabz that showcases authentic stories of farmer 
resilience. Each episode is a tribute to our courageous farmers, and we pride ourselves in sharing 
their stories with the world through our digital platform.  
Furthermore, the Sarsabz Pakistan App, launched by Fatima Fertilizer, serves as a 
comprehensive digital platform for the farming community. It provides timely farm 
advisory services and information updates to help farmers enhance crop yield and 
productivity. The App has now surpassed 800,000+ downloads, making it the country’s 
fastest-growing agriculture mobile application. 
Adding to this, our flagship application, Sarsabz Asaan, empowers our customers and dealers by 
facilitating online orders, e-banking payments, and order tracking, eliminating the need for physical 
interaction. Since its launch in 2023, the App has made substantial inroads with our customers and 
through this platform, we have recorded a staggering Rs.500 Billion Sales.  
Our rural women program, Sarsabz Tabeer, continues to strengthen our commitment 
to empowering female farmers across Pakistan. Built on four foundational pillars—
Education, Health, Finance, and Skill Development—the program aims to create long-
term, meaningful change in rural communities. 
Our overarching vision is to build a Sarsabz Pakistan, recognizing the pivotal role we 
play in supporting the backbone of the country's economy. By bringing prosperity to farmers and 
their families, villages, cities, and ultimately the nation of Pakistan, we believe in fostering positive 
and impactful change.

Case 1 – Salam Kissan 2026 
In 2019, we wanted to celebrate our farmers, acknowledge their efforts and recognize them as our 
partners in success. After providing farmers with the best-specialized products, we wanted to cater 
to their need of appreciation in the form of Farmers Day. 
Salam Kissan was celebrated on 18th December and a 360° campaign was launched around it. Over 
the years, the initiative has grown significantly and eventually resulted in the Government of 
Pakistan officially recognizing 18th December as National Kissan Day. 
Today, Salam Kissan has become one of Pakistan's most recognizable purpose-led initiatives and has 
strengthened Sarsabz's position as the brand that genuinely cares for and celebrates the farming 
community. 
In 2026, we want to make Salam Kissan bigger and better by taking the movement to the next level 
and strengthening our ownership of the cause. 
Task 
Develop a concept and a 360° integrated campaign for Salam Kissan that: 
• Celebrates Pakistan's farmers and their contribution towards the country.  
• Strengthens Sarsabz's position as the pioneer and champion of the Salam Kissan movement.  
• Connects with both rural and urban audiences, especially the younger generation.  
• Creates relevance beyond a single day and transforms Salam Kissan into a nationwide 
movement.  
Agencies are encouraged to present ideas across all relevant touchpoints including ATL, BTL, PR, 
Events and On-Ground Activations. 
Goal 
To maximize awareness of Salam Kissan and National Kissan Day by touching both rural and urban 
audiences and making people understand: 
• The importance of farmers in our daily lives.  
• The contribution of agriculture towards Pakistan's economy.  
• The role played by Sarsabz in celebrating and empowering the farming community.

Case 2: Sarsabz Brand Audit & Tactical Campaign Development 
Sarsabz was launched in 2011 with the vision of helping progressive farmers achieve greater yields 
through high-quality, research-based fertilizer solutions. Over the years, the brand has evolved from 
being a product-focused brand to a farmer-centric brand that celebrates, supports and partners with 
the farming community. 
Through multiple campaigns and initiatives including 10 Feesad Aur, Salam Kissan, Sarsabz Kahani, 
Sarsabz Tabeer and Sarsabz Ki Jeet, Sarsabz has built strong brand equity and established itself as a 
trusted name in the fertilizer category. Today, Sarsabz stands on three key brand pillars: Experts, 
Caring, and One-Stop Solution. 
One of the brand's strongest assets is its functional yield claim, backed by more than 500 
demonstration plots and carried in the promise "10 feesad se bhe ziada izaafi paidawaar." This 
promise recently found real-world validation through Sarsabz Ki Jeet, which leveraged Sarsabz 
farmers' wins in the Punjab Government Wheat Yield Competition to prove the brand's claim 
through actual farmer results — reinforcing the belief that when farmers win, Sarsabz wins. 
As we continue to strengthen the brand and prepare for the next phase of growth, we are looking 
for a creative partner who can critically evaluate our existing brand ecosystem — including what 
we've built through Sarsabz Ki Jeet — and identify opportunities to further enhance Sarsabz's 
relevance and leadership. 
Task 
Conduct a comprehensive review and audit of the current Sarsabz brand assets, communication and 
campaigns, including an assessment of Sarsabz Ki Jeet alongside the brand's other platforms. Based 
on the audit, recommend a tactical campaign idea for Sarsabz, That talks about the functional 
aspects of the brand and pushes the "10 feesad se bhe ziada izaafi paidawaar" promise among 
farmers. 
Based on your assessment, recommend: 
• Opportunities to strengthen the brand positioning. 
• Areas of improvement in current communication. 
• New creative territories that Sarsabz can explore. 
• Opportunities to create stronger emotional and functional connections with farmers. 
• Recommendations to improve brand consistency across different touchpoints. 
• Whether Sarsabz Ki Jeet's farmer wins can be used as authentic proof within the tactical 
campaign for the yield promise. 
• An identified brand or business challenge. 
• Ways to strengthen Sarsabz's leadership position. 
• Ways to create engagement with farmers and relevant stakeholders. 
Agencies are encouraged to present ideas that have the ability to be executed across multiple 
touchpoints, including ATL, BTL, Digital, Dealer/Trade and On-Ground channels.
