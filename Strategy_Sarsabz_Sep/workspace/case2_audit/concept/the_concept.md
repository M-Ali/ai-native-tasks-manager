# Sarsabz — Campaign Concept (Case 2)

**Dus Feesad Aur. Khet Gawah Hai.**
*Ten percent more. The field is the witness.*

Tactical campaign for "10 feesad se bhi ziada izaafi paidawaar" (brief p13), built on the Big Idea
*Sarsabz proves itself on a field like yours*. Method: `campaign-concept`. Working draft, 15 September 2026.

---

## The stated assumptions

- **No budget or pitch date is given in the brief.** The executions are sized to show the idea travels, not costed.
- **Plot numbers are a proposal, not a fact.** One paired plot per Ki Jeet district (34) in Rabi 2026-27 is the starting
  scale suggested here; Fatima's agronomy team sets the real number.
- **The 10% claim needs clearing for advertising.** Its substantiation sits in Fatima's demo-plot data, which was not
  supplied. That is a question for the client, not an assumption this concept makes.

## 1. What we found

Sarsabz makes the loudest yield promise in the category and proves it almost nowhere: 10 of its 11 "10%" films show nothing but
the badge. Engro, the only competitor with real owned video, has made itself the farmer's adviser: it answers a farmer
objection in 58% of its 110 uploads, against Sarsabz's 6%. No brand in 200 uploads shows a result on a real field beside the
old way. Meanwhile 1,433 farmer comments don't question whether NP and CAN work. They ask how to use them without waste: 220
comments on mixing alone.

## 2. What it means

The tension is the switch, not the product. A farmer moving from DAP and urea to NP and CAN is betting a season on advice
he can't check, and the advice he gets contradicts itself, sometimes against Fatima's own field practice. The category
avoids this tension for structural reasons. Urea and DAP are near-commodities, so Engro competes on service around the bag.
Fatima has had almost no rival on its own products, so Sarsabz has never had to prove them.

## 3. The idea

| | |
|---|---|
| **Asset** | The only CAN and 93% of NP in Pakistan, 500+ demo plots, Ki Jeet winners in 34 of 40 districts. Engro holds 6% of NP and no CAN. |
| **Tension** | Farmers won't bet a season on a switch nobody has shown them how to make without waste. |
| **Territory** | Proof on a real field: a result beside the old way. |
| **Proposition** | Sarsabz puts its NP and CAN beside your usual DAP and urea on a field like yours, weighs both, and publishes the result and the recipe, whatever it shows. |
| **Line** | **Dus Feesad Aur. Khet Gawah Hai.** |
| **Mechanic** | *Do Khet* (two fields) · *Kaanta Din* (weighing day) · *Nuskha* (the recipe) |

**The line revives what Sarsabz already owns.** "Dus Feesad Aur" ran as a 2018 wheat film and a 2021 rice TVC with 1.8M
views, and has been dormant since. "Khet Gawah Hai" (the field is the witness) makes the field, not the brand, the one
making the claim. That's the answer to farmers who suspect paid advice.

**Lines considered:**
- **Pehle Dekho, Phir Daalo** (see first, then apply): clear, but new and ownable by anyone.
- **Revive "Khaad Muft He Samjho"**: owned (1.1M views, 2022), but a price line walks into the fight Engro is running with
  "Kam Kharch, Zyada Paidawar".
- **Keep "10% Se Bhi Ziyada Izaafi Paidawaar"**: the brief's wording, and the badge that already goes unproven.

## 4. Why only Sarsabz

Engro could film a demo plot and even say "Khet Gawah Hai". But its field would show urea and DAP, because it holds 6% of NP
and no CAN. FFC has no owned video channel at all. Only Sarsabz can put NP and CAN on the winning side of the comparison,
and it already holds 500+ plots and Ki Jeet winners to start from.

## 5. How it travels

**The mechanic.** *Do Khet*: one farmer, one soil, split down the middle, his usual DAP and urea against Sarsabz NP and CAN.
*Kaanta Din*: both halves weighed on camera in front of neighbours, with an Agriculture Department or university officer
reading the scale. *Nuskha*: what replaced DAP, how many bags, mixed with what, on which irrigation, printed and filmed with
the result.

**Across the brief's five channels:**

| Channel | Execution | What it answers |
|---|---|---|
| ATL | "Do Khet" TV and DVC: the scale and the number. 15s for the result, 60s for the recipe. Radio reads district results at harvest. | Farmers are exposed to terrestrial TV and radio (brief p7) |
| BTL | A Nuskha card in every bag. The Nitrophos sticker code moves from the prize draw to proof: SMS it for your district's result. | 220 comments on what mixes with what |
| Digital | Kaanta Din live on TikTok (1.2M) and YouTube (396K); shorts point to the long recipe film; the app finds the nearest plot and lets farmers log their own. | Farmers ask strangers, and suspect paid answers |
| Dealer / Trade | A Khet Gawah Hai board at every dealer and AgriMart: this district's plot, both weights, the recipe. | Farmers "replicate best practices shared by fellow farmers" (p7) |
| On-ground | Kaanta Din field days hosted by Ki Jeet winners; the Sarsabz Kissan Convention becomes results day. | "High believability in word of mouth publicity" (p7) |

**Across a year:**

| When | Stage | Work |
|---|---|---|
| Oct – Nov 2026 | Sowing | Plots planted in Ki Jeet districts; launch "Do Khet" 15s, radio, dealer boards |
| Dec 2026 – Jan 2027 | First irrigations | Nuskha push when mixing questions peak |
| Feb – Mar 2027 | Growth | Farmer diaries from the plots, week by week |
| Apr – May 2027 | Harvest | Kaanta Din live; district results, including any under 10% |
| Jun – Sep 2027 | Kharif | Rice, cotton and maize plots, including Sindh rice, where Ki Jeet has no proof yet |
| Oct 2027 | Month twelve | Ki Jeet with published inputs; next Rabi with farmer-logged plots from the app |

**How we'll know it worked** (the brief asks for "a practical and measurable approach", p14):

| Kind | Measure | Baseline |
|---|---|---|
| Leading | Plots planted and published; Kaanta Din attendance; sticker-code look-ups; mixing questions answered | New, set at launch |
| Lagging | NP and CAN offtake in plot districts against matched districts without plots | Client sales data, requested |
| Brand | Top-of-mind and spontaneous awareness in the next brand health wave; farmers naming Sarsabz, not just "Nitrophos" | TOM 22% (p29); Sarsabz named in 15 of 1,433 comments |

## 6. What it asks of Fatima

1. **Publish every plot result, including those under 10%.** A campaign called "the field is the witness" can't hide a field.
2. **Clear the 10% claim for advertising** with the plot data behind it.
3. **Have Fatima agronomy sign the Nuskha**, so field staff, dealers and creators give one answer on mixing.
4. **Move the Nitrophos sticker code** from the prize draw to verification.
5. **Agree an independent adjudicator** for the weigh-ins.

If Fatima won't publish results that fall short, don't run "Khet Gawah Hai". Run the corrected Ki Jeet winner stories under
"Dus Feesad Aur" alone, and say plainly that it's a promise, not proof.

## The six tests

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Swap | Engro's field would show urea and DAP. The line is false in any other brand's mouth. |
| Asset | The only CAN, 93% of NP, 500+ plots: structural, not tone. |
| Deletion | Remove the audit and there's no reason to choose proof over advice. They're one argument. |
| Department | Agronomy, legal, sales and the app team must act. It asks Fatima to do something, not only say it. |
| Month twelve | New crops, districts and farmer-logged plots every season. A platform, not a launch. |
| Room | "Ten percent more, the field is the witness." A farmer can repeat it. |
