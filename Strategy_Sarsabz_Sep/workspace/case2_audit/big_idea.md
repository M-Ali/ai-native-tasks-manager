# Sarsabz — The Big Idea (Case 2)

Method: `big-idea` skill. Three single-line insights from three bodies of evidence, combined into one organising thought.
Compiled 15 September 2026. Every line points to its evidence; the brand insight is provisional (see below).

---

## CONSUMER INSIGHT — relevance

**Farmers don't doubt that Nitrophos and CAN work, but they won't bet a season on a switch nobody has shown them how to make without waste.**

Evidence (1,433 YouTube comments on independent agronomy channels, `comments/analysis.md`):
- Mixing and compatibility is the largest subject in the file: 220 comments, 83 about NP or CAN, 33 putting Nitrophos beside Engro Zabardast.
- *"Zabrdast urea ma zink ha aur np ma phosphorus ha to akthy dalen gy to zink phosphorus to fix ho jay ge"* (Zabardast has zinc, NP has phosphorus; put them together and they lock up).
- (Translated from Urdu) "How many bags of Sarsabz Nitrophos give the same result as one DAP, or can it not replace DAP?"
- *"Fatima fertilizer walon ne NP+CAN+ZINC SULFATE mila ke chatta kiya hai result bhe behar hai aor aap bol rahai in ko aapus main na milaain"* (Fatima's people mixed NP + CAN + zinc sulphate and the result was better, and you say don't mix them).
- No comment mentions the 10% promise. One asks *"Can gawara say paidawar mien izafa hota hy kia"* (does CAN increase yield at all?).

Limits: self-selected viewers of agronomy channels; channels and dates not supplied; not a measure of what "most farmers" think.

Candidates considered: "Farmers trust the result, not the recipe" (too abstract) · "Farmers fear waste more than they want yield" (not evidenced as a comparison) · "Farmers ask strangers what the maker should tell them" (true, but a channel observation, kept as the channel insight).

## CATEGORY INSIGHT — distinctiveness

**Engro has made itself the farmer's adviser and Sarsabz makes the loudest yield promise, but no brand shows the result on a real field beside the old way.**

Evidence (200 coded owned-YouTube uploads, 15 Sep 2024 – 15 Sep 2026, `scan/scan_findings.md`):
- Engro answers a farmer objection in 58% of its 110 uploads; Sarsabz in 6% of its 90.
- Engro: 44 advisory uploads, 31 with a named agronomist, soil testing (Mitti Mahir), a cost-per-acre podcast, retail centres and an app.
- Sarsabz makes the yield claim 15 times; Engro makes it 0 times. 10 of Sarsabz's 11 "10%" films offer nothing but the badge.
- FFC Sona, Bubber Sher and Agritech have no official YouTube channel.

Why the category behaves this way: urea and DAP are near-commodities, so Engro competes on what comes with the bag: a zinc coating, advice, a service. Sarsabz sells products no rival makes at scale (the only CAN and 93% of NP), so proof is its natural ground, and it's the ground it isn't using. The space isn't empty because it failed: Sarsabz Ki Jeet, the closest thing to field proof, carries Sarsabz's biggest product films (8.2M and 7.5M views).

## BRAND INSIGHT — ownership · PROVISIONAL

**Sarsabz holds proof no rival can produce — the only CAN and 93% of NP in Pakistan, 500+ demo plots, Ki Jeet winners in 34 districts — but keeps it off screen, so farmers know the product and forget the name.**

Evidence:
- Fatima Group holds 93% of NP (Fatima Fertilizer 51% + Pak Arab 42%) and is the sole CAN producer; Engro holds 6% of NP (CCP Table 11, draft, NFDC 2022-23).
- The yield promise is "documented by… 500+ demo plots across Pakistan" (brief p5). No plot appears in 90 uploads.
- Ki Jeet films: "40 mein se 34 azlaa mein hui Sarsabz kissanon ki jeet" (wins in 34 of 40 districts; video descriptions).
- Farmers name CAN 193 times and NP 147 times, but Sarsabz 15 times (comments).

Where the brief and the evidence disagree:
- Brief: the brand has "evolved from being a product-focused brand to a farmer-centric brand" (p13). Evidence: farmers judge at product level, and Engro is winning there.
- Brief: 22% top-of-mind "exceeded competition's TOM awareness by 6%" (p29). Chart: that holds against Competitor 2 only; Competitor 1 is at 62%.

Provisional because the demo-plot dataset, current product-level share and the brand-health study's method and competitor names were not supplied.

## CHANNEL INSIGHT

**The switch is decided in agronomy creators' comment threads, where farmers ask strangers what the maker should be telling them, and suspect the answers are paid.**

---

## THE BIG IDEA

**Sarsabz proves itself on a field like yours.**

What it means: the 10% stops being a badge and becomes a result a farmer can see on a field like his, with the recipe that produced it: what replaced DAP, what it was mixed with, on which irrigation, on what soil.

Removal test: without the consumer insight it's demo-plot PR nobody asked for; without the category insight it's Engro's advice; without the brand insight any brand could promise a demo, but only Sarsabz makes the NP and CAN on the winning side of it.

## STRATEGY

Re-frame Sarsabz from a yield promise farmers take on trust to the proof they can see and copy, by putting its demo plots and winning farmers side by side with DAP + urea on fields like theirs, and publishing the recipe behind every result.

## COMMS PLATFORM

Position Sarsabz as **THE PROOF, NOT THE PROMISE.**

## WHY IT HOLDS

- **Relevant:** it answers the largest subject in the farmer conversation, how to switch without waste.
- **Distinctive:** Engro advises, Sarsabz asserts, and nobody in 200 uploads shows a result beside the old way.
- **Ownable:** only the maker of 93% of NP and all of CAN can put those products on the winning side of the comparison.

## ALTERNATIVES CONSIDERED

- **A. Sarsabz proves itself on a field like yours.** *Recommended.* Built on all three insights. Needs Fatima to publish plot results, including the ones that fell short.
- **B. The farmer's trusted adviser.** Engro's territory: 44 advisory uploads and 31 named experts. Sarsabz would arrive second in a space Engro already owns.
- **C. Saluting the farmer (Sarsabz Pakistan).** Where the brand talks today (Level 5, 30 of 90 uploads), and it's right for Salam Kissan. But farmers name Sarsabz 15 times in 1,433 comments; a salute doesn't sell a switch.

Swap test: Engro could film a demo plot, but it can't put NP and CAN on the winning side, with 6% of NP and no CAN.

## WHAT IT ASKS OF FATIMA

1. **Publish the plots.** Demo-plot results by crop, district and soil, including those under 10%.
2. **Correct Ki Jeet.** State winners' yields and inputs; drop "sabit kar diya… 10%"; verify before saying "all winners".
3. **One mixing answer.** An official NP and CAN compatibility and timing guide, so field staff and creators stop contradicting each other.
4. **Name the recipe.** Every proof film names the farmer, district, soil, inputs and maunds.
5. **Answer in the thread.** Staff the Sarsabz Pakistan App and comment replies with agronomists.

If Fatima won't publish plot results, the idea becomes an assertion again. Run Ki Jeet winner stories with corrected claims instead, and state the trade-off.

## MEDIA PRINCIPLES

- **Show the field, not the badge.** Every yield claim points to a named plot or farmer.
- **Short points to long.** 15-second cuts carry the result; the long cut carries the recipe and gets funded.
- **Be in the creator thread, disclosed.** Farmers suspect paid advice, so partnerships are labelled.
- **Reach is already there.** 1.2M on TikTok and 396K on YouTube; use it for proof, not prizes.
- **The dealer is the last field.** AgriMart and dealers carry the side-by-side at the point of sale.
