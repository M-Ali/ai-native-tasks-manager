Sarsabz Earns Triple Recognition at Dragons of Pakistan 2026

September 3, 2026 1 Mins READ SHARE

Sarsabz has earned three accolades at the Dragons of Pakistan 2026, recognizing the creativity and effectiveness of its campaigns across content, loyalty, and marketing.

  • Bronze Dragon – Content Creation: Salam Kissan
  • Black Dragon – Loyalty & Acquisition: Sarsabz Ki Jeet
  • Black Dragon – Marketing Discipline: Faaslon Ko Takalluf — Ramadan Campaign featuring Atif Aslam

The Dragons of Pakistan are among the country’s leading marketing and communications awards, recognizing creative and effective campaigns that deliver meaningful impact across the industry.

Our business lines include fertilizer, energy, sugar, mining, venture capital, information technology, real estate, trading, textile, packaging, agribusinesses, cosmetics, cement and healthcare.
