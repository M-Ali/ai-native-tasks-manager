Unmatched comments are mostly short affirmations, greetings and off-topic chatter that carry no codeable subject. Stating coverage is what makes the matched counts credible.

## Sentiment method

Explicit positive/negative markers within 90 characters of a brand mention, with negated positives ("not good", "sahi nahi") counted as negative. This is **directional, not measured**: sarcasm, code-switching and Roman Urdu spelling variants all defeat it, and most mentions carry no explicit marker at all. Use it to say which brands are discussed warmly, never to publish a sentiment percentage as fact.
