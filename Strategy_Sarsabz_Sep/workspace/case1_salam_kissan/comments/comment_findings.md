# Salam Kissan on YouTube: what commenters said (2019-2025)

**Base: 1,572 comments on 18 Salam Kissan uploads (≥380,000 views each). 56 are the channel's own replies, leaving 1,516 audience
comments from 1,155 accounts.** Collected with yt-dlp on 16 Sep 2026 (`collect.py`), all read. The counts come from `analyse.py`, and
every coded comment is listed in `comment_codes.csv`. Translations are ours and labelled; the original text is in the CSV.

## What this corpus can and cannot tell you

- It is self-selected YouTube commenters on brand uploads, not a sample of farmers or of urban Pakistan. It cannot give a
  rural/urban split, an age split or a sentiment percentage. Commenters' identity is only what they state.
- **Comments sit on four films.** The 2023 anthem has 829 (53%), the 2019 anthem 300, the 2021 anthem 177 and the 2022 anthem 116.
  The eight 2025 uploads have 61 audience comments between them (6-11 each).
- **The 2023 thread was driven by more than the film.** It contains:
  - **31 channel replies** telling winners to email their details ("Congratulations on winning! Please email your complete
    details… to sarsabzofficialpk@gmail.com")
  - **8 commenters talking about the prize** ("Thank you Sarsabz Fertilizer for this amazing gift i received today", "Done hope
    to win")
  - **4 congratulations** on the channel reaching 100K subscribers
  - **24 visitors** who say they came from the Ducky Bhai or "Shehr Main Dehat" vlogs ("Who is here after ducky Bahi vlog", "Here
    on Sheher may Dehat recommendation")
  - **Long, formal tributes posted in near-identical wording** by several accounts (e.g. "What an impressive effort by the team…",
    4 copies from 2 accounts)

  The rules of that contest aren't in the video description; ask the client.
- **2025:** four accounts that commented on 5 or more of the eight uploads wrote 35 of the 61 audience comments. One of them
  posted the same "Salam Kissan Sarsabz Pakistan 🇵🇰🌾 Dil se shukriya…" text 6 times.
- **2019:** one account replied "Fatima Group is Fraud Gang" 23 times on the anthem. The corpus gives no reason for it.

## What commenters say

**1. The anthems are what people respond to, and they respond to the music.** 103 comments talk about the song, lyrics, voice or
music: 56 on the 2019 film and 27 on the 2021 film. Nothing comparable appears on the 2025 shorts.
- "Heart touching lyrics, and a very emotional tune." (2019, 39 likes)
- "Heard a beautiful song after a long time "Just Love it and repeat mode on"" (2019, 20 likes)
- "magical background voice of Mai Dhaii" (2021, 15 likes)
- "یہ گانا جتنی بار سن لو، ہمیشہ نیا ہی لگتا ہے… میں گانے نہیں سنتی لیکن یہ گانا واقعی میں رونگٹے کھڑے کردیتا ہے" (2019, 9 likes;
  our translation: "however many times you hear this song it feels new… I don't listen to songs but this one really gives
  goosebumps")

**2. Farmers and farmers' children speak for themselves in the comments, though the films rarely let them.** Quoted as signal, not
counted:
- "I feel proud because my father is farmer." (2019, 123 likes, the most-liked comment on the 2019 film)
- "I'm daughter of farmer. My grandfather was a farmer. And I'm proud of it." (2019, 8 likes)
- "We love kissan because my father is farmer Wo mehnat krta hn or ham aj parh raha hn" (2023, 9 likes; our translation of the
  Urdu part: "he works hard and today we are studying")
- "Ma kaysan hu bht bora hal hai mera kisi hakomat nay humayn izat nhi di" (2022; our translation: "I am a farmer, my condition is very bad,
  no government has given us respect")

**3. People from outside farming write in too, usually by saying they aren't farmers.** Signal, not counted:
- "Although I am not a farmers but every Sarsabz ad is mind blowing!" (2021, 18 likes)

**4. Complaints are few and specific. They are about fertilizer supply, prices and respect, and none is aimed at the film.** Picked
by reading: **19 of 1,516** (6 on the 2023 film, 9 on 2022, 3 on 2019, 1 on 2021). Too few to generalise; quoted as signal:
- "کسانوں کو کھاد نہیں مل رہی یہ کسی کو نظر نہیں ائے گا کیا کسان ڈے منانے سے کسانوں کی مشکلات دور ہو سکتی ہیں ؟؟؟" (2023; our
  translation: "farmers aren't getting fertilizer, nobody will see that. Can celebrating Kissan Day solve farmers' problems???")
- "کھاد کے لیے پریشان ہیں کمپنی کوئی اسانیاں پیدا کرے" (2023, reply; our translation: "…worried about fertilizer, the company should
  make things easier")
- "کاش اس ملک میں کسان کی قدر ہوتی" (2022, 38 likes; our translation: "if only farmers were valued in this country")
- "khaad nayaab ho cuki hy" (2022; our translation: "fertilizer has become unavailable")
- "Make sure  the fertilizers of all kinds should not be black marketed" (2022)
- "Kissan ka zikr to milta hy but just siyasi byanaat main, sasti khaad, acha beej, fixed market prices… farmer ka haq hy" (2019;
  our translation: "farmers get mentioned, but only in political statements; cheap fertilizer, good seed, fixed market prices… are the
  farmer's right")

**5. One call for more than a day.** "We need to make this permanent not temporary. They are us and we are them." (2019 anthem,
87 likes). It is one comment, not a pattern.

**6. The 2019 anthem was picked up by Indian viewers during India's farmer protests.** 19 comments on it mention India or Bangladesh
(pattern count, which includes a few passing mentions), e.g. "Who is here after injustice to indian farmers" (122 likes, the
second most-liked comment on the film), and one reply pointing out the upload date: "it's Pakistani song about Pakistan farmers not
Indian farmers".

**7. The 2023 film's comments mention the floods.** 2023 commenters write about the flood the film depicts, e.g. "This video made
me tear up as I realized that the presence of farmers can be felt everywhere in our daily lives." (61 likes). Contest-era wording:
read with the caveat above.

## What the corpus does not show

- **Product mentions are rare, and none ties a product to the day's message.** They are: "Thanks for your support sarsabz urea." (2022);
  "From the landlord family, I really better know the importance of sarsabz fertilizer in our lives.." (2023); "سرسبز کھاد پاؤ ۔۔تے
  خوشحال ہو جاؤ" (2023, our translation: "use Sarsabz fertilizer and prosper"); a former employee ("I was a part of this company,
  fatima fertiliser sarsabz urea", 2019); and three near-identical 2023 comments, one in Urdu, citing a 10% extra yield.
- No comment says what commenters did on 18 December, or that they took part in anything beyond commenting and the contest.
- Nothing here measures awareness of Kissan Day or whether people link it to Sarsabz. That needs the client's brand-health data.
