# Case 1 — Salam Kissan 2026: evidence so far

Working file, started 16 September 2026. **Status: evidence gathering.** No insight or idea is decided yet. Only retrieved or
clearly-labelled reported facts are recorded; unverified claims are marked as such and not used.

**Retrieved** = read by us from the source. **Reported** = from a search summary or secondary page. Marked where it matters.

---

## 1. What the brief asks (p12)

`uv run sarsabz-pitch case1` prints these, page-traced.

- **Task:** a concept and 360° integrated campaign that (1) celebrates farmers' contribution to the country, (2) strengthens
  Sarsabz as pioneer and champion of the movement, (3) connects with rural *and* urban audiences, especially the young, and
  (4) creates relevance beyond a single day and turns Salam Kissan into a nationwide movement.
- **Goal:** maximise awareness of Salam Kissan and National Kissan Day, and make people understand the importance of farmers in daily
  life, agriculture's contribution to the economy, and Sarsabz's role.
- **Touchpoints:** ATL, BTL, PR, Events, On-Ground Activations.
- **The 2026 ambition in the client's words:** "bigger and better", "the next level", and **"strengthening our ownership of the cause"**.

## 2. Seven years of Salam Kissan on @Sarsabz (retrieved, coded)

76 uploads, 2019-2025, 78.4M views. Coded after reading every thumbnail. Full tables: `archive/archive_findings.md`;
codes: `archive/archive_codes.csv`.

| Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **It lives on one day.** | 60 of 76 uploads (79%) and 80% of views fall between 1 Dec and 15 Jan. |
| **The farmer is spoken about, rarely speaks.** | A farmer speaks in 9 of 76 uploads (2.3M views, outside product films). Celebrities and politicians speak in 30. 81% of views go to films where nobody speaks. |
| **Four long anthem films carry most of the views.** | Uploads coded as anthem films carry 57% of views: 2019 2.1M (210s), 2021 13.1M (210s), 2022 6.5M (169s), 2023 22.6M (255s). No upload of that length and title pattern appears in 2024. In 2025 the "Salam Kissan 2025 | A Tribute" upload is a 41-second short (5.4M). Coded from thumbnails, titles and descriptions; not watched, so whether the 2025 short uses the anthem is not verified. |
| **2024 almost disappeared.** | Two 15-second farmer salutes (about 2K views each), plus an event recap. The biggest 2024 upload was the UNDP corporate film. |
| **Uploads addressed to urban viewers ask them to thank farmers or not waste food.** | Verbatim titles: "Let's Pledge Not To Waste Food" (2020), "Thank you, Kissan!" (2020), "How would you feel if this was your reality?" and "Will you continue to ignore the hardwork of those who provide for you?" (2022), "Behind every meal is a farmer's hard work." (2025, food blogger, 4.0M). 28 uploads coded as addressed to urban viewers. |
| **2020 was the peak of borrowed voices.** | 10 celebrities (Afridi, Wasim Akram, Muniba Mazari, Reema Khan…) and 5 ministers "celebrating Kissan Day on 18th December". |
| **2025 went synthetic.** | Five 3D/AI animated farmer shorts (about 1M views each). |

Limits: owned YouTube only. TikTok, where the brief's awards were won, isn't readable logged-out (probe failed 16 Sep).

## 2b. What YouTube commenters said (retrieved, all read)

1,572 comments on the 18 Salam Kissan uploads with ≥380,000 views; 1,516 from the audience (1,155 accounts). Full write-up with
verbatims and limits: `comments/comment_findings.md`. Counts: `comments/analyse.py` → `comments/comment_codes.csv`.

| Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Comments sit on the four anthems.** | 2023: 829, 2019: 300, 2021: 177, 2022: 116. The eight 2025 uploads: 61 audience comments together. |
| **Commenters respond to the music.** | 103 comments on song, lyrics, voice or music (56 on 2019, 27 on 2021, none on 2025 uploads). |
| **Farmers and their children self-identify.** | "I feel proud because my father is farmer." (2019, 123 likes). Quoted as signal, not counted. |
| **Complaints are few: fertilizer supply, price, respect.** | 19 of 1,516, picked by reading. "کسانوں کو کھاد نہیں مل رہی… کیا کسان ڈے منانے سے کسانوں کی مشکلات دور ہو سکتی ہیں ؟؟؟" (2023). |
| **The 2023 thread is not organic.** | 31 channel "Congratulations on winning" replies, 8 prize comments, 4 "100K subscribers" congratulations, and 24 visitors from the Ducky Bhai / "Shehr Main Dehat" vlogs. |
| **2025 comments are thin and repetitive.** | 4 accounts active on 5+ of the 8 uploads wrote 35 of 61. |

Not shown by the corpus: rural/urban or age split, awareness, attribution of the day to Sarsabz.

## 3. Who owns the day now (retrieved unless marked)

- **The Islamabad administration runs its own "Salam Kissan" campaign.** The ICT Agriculture Department decorated the capital with
  banners, streamers and billboards for Farmers Day, citing agriculture at 24% of GDP. *Reported* from the Daily Times summary. The
  article itself is Cloudflare-blocked and **undated**, and Sarsabz isn't named in the summary.
- **The 2024 national day was observed without the brand's name.** The Nation, 19 Dec 2024: "5th National Farmers' Day" observed
  through events by the Kashtkar Dost Foundation and All Pakistan Kissan Ittehad. It credits "Fatima Group" for proposing the day in
  2019. **Neither Sarsabz nor Salam Kissan is mentioned.**
- **Fatima still runs the flagship event.** The sixth Kissan Day was in Islamabad with Federal Minister Rana Tanveer Hussain, the FAO
  representative and Kissan Ittehad (ProPakistani, 8 Jan 2025, press release).
- **"Officially recognised" is unverified.** The brief (p7, p12) says the Federal Government recognises 18 December. The News (16 Dec
  2020) calls Fatima "the moving force" but cites no government notification. No notification has been found.

**Other brands on YouTube (retrieved; `scan/scan_findings.md`):**
- **Syngenta Pakistan** posts "Kisan Day" films every December: 2023 with #MaiKisanHun (60s, 399K views), and 2024 titled "کسان کا عالمی دن" (70s, 689K).
- **JPL Pakistan** (2020) and **Rizq Foods** (2025) title their Kissan Day videos "Salam Kissan" / "Salam Kisan".
- Barket Fertilizers, Soby Agro Machinery, BaKhabar Kissan and LIMS post small Kissan Day videos.
- The only FFC trace is a third-party upload of a regional manager's speech. No Engro Kissan Day campaign was found.
- Express News and BOL credit Fatima by name in Kissan Day programmes (2022, 2024). GNN, SAMAA, Aaj and Neo titles name no brand.
- One agri channel's title: "18 december kisan day sirf tv show per kisan Haqeeqat main rull gia pakistan ka kisan" (2022).

**Open question for the analysis:** in the two independent reports found, the day is observed or reused without the Sarsabz or Salam Kissan name. Two reports are not enough to say how widespread this is. Brand-health attribution data (asked for below) would settle it.

## 4. What Salam Kissan has achieved (and what can't be claimed)

- **Awards (brief p9-10, retrieved):** The Drum 2023 and 2025 (UGC for Social Change on TikTok, Social Media for Good, Cause-Led
  Influencer), Dragons of Asia 2025 Silver, and Pakistan Digital Awards 2020-2026.
- **TikTok scale is unverified. Don't use it.** A search summary claimed "1.5M+ UGC videos, 4.19B+ views, ~19.8M reach" with no
  traceable source. Fatima's own Dawn advertorial on the 2025 Drum wins (11 Dec 2025) says only **"hundreds of reels were
  submitted from rural and urban communities"**. The ProPakistani release (25 Nov 2025) gives no metrics. Ask the client for the
  real TikTok data.
- **"Life Without Kissan" 2022, "35.5 million people"** is *reported* from a search summary. Unverified.
