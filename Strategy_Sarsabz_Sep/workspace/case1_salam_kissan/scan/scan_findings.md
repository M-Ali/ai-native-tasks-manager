# Who else marks Kissan Day on YouTube (retrieved 16 Sep 2026)

**Method.** `search.py` ran 7 YouTube searches (60 results each) → `search_raw.csv`, 254 unique videos, 168 with Kissan/Farmers Day or
Salam Kissan in the title. Titles read; brand, institution, media and critic channels picked, then full metadata fetched
(`others.py` → `others.csv`, 36 videos, 3 unavailable). **Limits:** YouTube search ranking is not a census; TikTok, Facebook and
Instagram are not covered; views on brand channels are mostly paid weight, not popularity. Translations are ours.

## Sarsabz dominates the search, but not alone

- **Sarsabz:** 43 of the 168 matching results, 46.3M views.
- **Indian "Kisan Diwas" (23 Dec) status videos crowd the same searches.** e.g. Yaariyan Production "happy farmers day 2025 ||
  happy national farmers day" (2.2M views). The English term "Farmers Day" is shared with India's day.

  (our translation: "Kissan Day on 18 December is only on TV shows; in reality Pakistan's farmer is ruined"). 881 views. It matches
